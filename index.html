Dear reader,

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Boston University passionate about AI, robotics, and building things that make life easier, smarter, or more fun. I enjoy working on projects that combine engineering, creativity, and impact — whether that's developing autonomous robots, building digital tools, or creating new user experiences.

At BU, I've been working on a startup project called VibeWake, which explores how tech can reshape how we wake up and start our day — combining behavioral science, smart hardware, and app development. It's a passion project that pushes me to think like both an engineer and an entrepreneur.

Before college, I served as team captain of FIRST Tech Challenge Team 3371, where I led the design and programming of competition-ready robots, implemented computer vision systems, and secured sponsorships to keep our team moving. I also worked on the business and outreach side of a FIRST Robotics Competition team, managing a $50K+ budget and building community partnerships.

"I bring both a technical foundation and a business mindset."

Beyond robotics, I've had hands-on experience in:

  • Web development JavaScript, Node.js, C++
  • UX/UI design Figma, user testing
  • Marketing & logistics campaign strategy, team leadership
  • Research & data MATLAB, Excel, Python

I'm excited by how AI and robotics can be used ethically to solve real-world problems in education, health, and everyday life. I'm always looking to learn, build, and collaborate on something meaningful.

Let's connect! — Shiqi
