      Dear reader,

      I'm a Computer Engineering student at Boston University passionate about AI, robotics, and building things that make life easier, smarter, or more fun. I enjoy working on projects that combine engineering, creativity, and impact — whether that's developing autonomous robots, building digital tools, or creating new user experiences.

      At BU, I've been working on a startup project called VibeWake, which explores how tech can reshape how we wake up and start our day — combining behavioral science, smart hardware, and app development. It's a passion project that pushes me to think like both an engineer and an entrepreneur.

      Before college, I served as team captain of FIRST Tech Challenge Team 3371, where I led the design and programming of competition-ready robots, implemented computer vision systems, and secured sponsorships to keep our team moving. I also worked on the business/outreach side of a FIRST Robotics Competition team, managing a $50K+ budget and building community partnerships.

      Beyond robotics, I've had hands-on experience in:

      • Web development (JavaScript, Node.js, C++)
      • UX/UI design (Figma, user testing)
      • Marketing and logistics (campaign strategy, team leadership)
      • Research and data analysis (MATLAB, Excel, Python)

      I'm excited by how AI and robotics can be used ethically to solve real-world problems in education, health, and everyday life. I bring both a technical foundation and a business mindset — and I'm always looking to learn, build, and collaborate on something meaningful.
